About R01

Good to know.

Meet R01

What makes R01 different?

Health tracking and voice recording, together. Track heart rate, blood oxygen, sleep and activity. Turn recordings into notes, to-dos and calendar entries. One ring for your body and your thoughts.

Recording & AI

How do I start and stop recording?

Double-press the button to record. A gentle vibration and white light confirm the start. Double-press again to stop. Pause and cancel aren't supported. Delete recordings after transferring them to your phone.

Can I record without a phone or internet?

Yes. The ring stores up to 2 hours of audio for later transfer to your phone. AI transcription and summaries need internet access.

Can I record outdoors? From how far?

Works at 1–5 meters, outdoors too. Beyond 5 meters isn't supported.

Which languages are supported?

Mandarin, Chinese dialects, English and mixed Chinese–English speech.

What does AI create? Can I edit it?

Transcripts, summaries, speaker labels, action items and calendar entries. To-dos and calendar entries support editing, reminders and sync.

How much transcription is included?

The basic subscription includes 300 transcription minutes a month. After that, you can still save recordings to your phone, but extra minutes cannot currently be transcribed.

Privacy & compatibility

Does my data stay in the cloud?

Recordings and health data stay on your phone and can be deleted. The cloud temporarily reads audio for transcription and summaries, but stores neither audio nor results. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

Which phones work with R01?

iOS at launch. Android planned for January 2027.

Fit & daily use

How do I find my size?

We'll send a paper sizing kit first. Find your fit, then tell us your ring size.

What does it track? Is it for diagnosis?

Heart rate, blood oxygen, sleep and activity—for everyday insight, not medical diagnosis.

Can I wash my hands or swim with it?

Handwashing and cold-water swimming are fine. Showers and hot-water swimming are not recommended. Tested by CTI to meet IPX8 requirements.

How do I charge it? How long does it last?

Use the charging case. A full charge takes about 90 minutes and lasts around 7 days, depending on use.

What are the finishes, material and weight?

Mist Silver or Shadow Black, in 306 stainless steel. Size 6, the smallest ring, weighs about 4.6 g.
