About R01

A little more to know.

Meet R01

How is R01 different from a health ring or a recording ring?

R01 combines health tracking and voice recording in one ring. It tracks heart rate, blood oxygen, sleep and activity, and organizes what you say into notes, to-dos and calendar entries—helping you manage both your body and your thoughts.

Recording & AI

How do I start and stop recording?

Double-press the ring button to start recording. A gentle vibration and white light confirm it has started. Double-press again to stop. Pausing and canceling are not supported; you can delete a recording after transferring it to your phone.

Can I record without my phone or an internet connection?

Yes. R01 records independently and can temporarily store up to 2 hours of audio on the ring for later transfer to your phone. AI transcription and summaries require an internet connection.

What is the recording range? Can I use it outdoors?

Recording is supported at distances of 1–5 meters, including outdoors. Distances beyond 5 meters are outside the supported range.

Which languages can it transcribe?

It supports Mandarin Chinese, Chinese dialects, English, and mixed Chinese–English speech.

What can it organize? Can I edit to-dos and calendar entries?

R01 supports transcription, AI summaries, speaker identification, and extraction of action items and calendar entries. You can edit the resulting to-dos and calendar entries, with reminders and syncing supported.

How much transcription is included each month? What if I use it all?

The basic subscription includes 300 minutes of transcription per month. Once the allowance is used, recordings can still be transferred to your phone and saved, but transcription beyond the allowance is not currently available.

Privacy & compatibility

Are recordings and health data stored in the cloud?

Recordings and health data are stored locally on your phone and can be deleted by you. AI transcription and summaries temporarily process audio in the cloud, but the cloud does not store your audio or retain the results. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

Which phones are supported?

iOS is supported at launch. Android support is planned for January 2027.

Wearing & daily use

How do I choose my ring size?

We will first send you a paper sizing kit. Use it to choose a comfortable size, then let us know your ring size.

What does it track? Can the data be used for diagnosis?

R01 tracks heart rate, blood oxygen, sleep and activity to help you understand your everyday physical state. Health data is for daily reference and must not be used for medical diagnosis.

Can I wear it while washing hands, showering or swimming?

You can wear it for handwashing and cold-water swimming. Wearing it while showering or swimming in hot water is not recommended. R01 has been professionally tested by CTI to meet IPX8 requirements.

How do I charge it, and how long does a full charge last?

Charge R01 in its charging case. A full charge takes about 90 minutes and lasts around 7 days; actual battery life varies with use.

Which finishes and material are available? How much does it weigh?

R01 comes in Mist Silver and Shadow Black, in 306 stainless steel. The smallest ring, size 6, weighs approximately 4.6 g.
